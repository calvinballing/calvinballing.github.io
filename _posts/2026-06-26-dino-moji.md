For our second child's second birthday, we are celebrating with a dinosaur-themed party, so I wrote a dinosaur puzzle.  The objective is to identify the dinosaur names (some are real, some fictional, some are modern avian dinosaurs, and one is dino-adjacent) from the emoji.  I've previously made Emoji puzzles for [fruit](/fruit-emoji), [bugs](/bug-emoji), and [bees](/bee-moji).
